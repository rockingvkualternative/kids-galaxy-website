YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kids Galaxy, an Android application project developed by Seven Ink Digital Developers Team (“we”, “us”), handles information in connection with the app.

1. Information and app operation

The app requires internet access for online features and advertising. The Android manifest in the reviewed app source disables Firebase Analytics collection and removes the Android advertising ID permission. The app source also includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK. We do not intentionally request a child’s precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone access in the reviewed manifest.

Features and data practices can change as the app is updated. If a future version introduces account creation, sign-in, cloud storage, or other information collection, this policy will be updated before or when those features are introduced.

2. Advertising and third-party services

Kids Galaxy includes Google Mobile Ads. Google and its service providers may process limited technical information (such as app, device, and ad-delivery signals) to provide, protect, and measure advertising, subject to their policies and applicable settings. The app source reviewed removes the Android advertising ID permission and disables Firebase Analytics collection. These controls do not mean that every third-party network signal is necessarily unavailable.

For more information, review Google’s Privacy Policy and Google AdMob information.

3. Children’s privacy

Kids Galaxy is intended for children and families. We aim to follow applicable child-protection and advertising requirements. Parents or guardians can contact us with questions or requests concerning information associated with a child. We will review and respond as required by applicable law.

4. Data security and retention

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the app and the information handled. We do not promise that internet transmission or storage is completely secure. Retention of information handled by third-party advertising providers is governed by their policies.

5. Your choices and parental requests

Parents or guardians may contact us to ask questions, raise a privacy concern, or request review/deletion of information that may have been provided through the app. We may need enough information to verify and process a request; please do not email sensitive personal information unless we ask for it.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when changes are made.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: seveninkdigitaldevelopers@gmail.com
